How Jerry Works With ShipPrimus

Jerry monitors your accounting inbox and works directly with ShipPrimus — your transportation management system. When a carrier sends an invoice, Jerry reads it, matches it to the correct load, validates the numbers, and carries the shipment through billing: proof of delivery, customer invoice, carrier payable, and QuickBooks sync. Jerry only pauses when your team needs to step in — and every pause comes with a clear email and a direct action link.

Watch

Monitors your invoice mailbox and picks up new carrier bills automatically.

Match

Links each invoice to the correct load, PRO, and customer in ShipPrimus.

Validate

Compares carrier charges against booked rates, lumpers, and approved extras.

Complete

Finishes invoicing, notifications, and follow-ups — or asks for help when needed.

Carrier Invoice Processing

Intelligent invoice intake

  • Reads carrier invoices from PDF and email attachments — including multi-invoice packets (e.g. LTL statement cover pages followed by freight bills).
  • Extracts load number, PRO, carrier name, invoice amount, and line items.
  • Matches each document to the correct shipment in ShipPrimus.
  • Flags amount mismatches, duplicate submissions, and unrecognized charges before billing proceeds.

Rate and amount validation

  • Compares the carrier bill to the booked carrier cost in ShipPrimus.
  • Handles lumper and accessorial charges with separate validation rules.
  • Auto-approves charges already reflected in the Primus carrier cost when totals match.
  • Pauses and notifies your team when numbers do not align.

Carrier bill upload

  • Uploads the carrier bill PDF to ShipPrimus as soon as the invoice amount is validated.
  • Enters carrier costs and issues the customer invoice through your Primus billing workflow.

Proof of Delivery (POD)

Jerry treats proof of delivery as a gatekeeper for billing. Where a POD is required, your team gets a clear notice — not a surprise later.

  • Detects when a load is missing a POD in ShipPrimus.
  • Uploads POD documents from carrier emails directly onto the booking.
  • Supports truckload, power-only, and drayage workflows with mode-appropriate rules.
  • For truckload shipments without a POD, Jerry sends structured follow-up reminders to the carrier on a business-day schedule before escalating to your team.
  • Retrieves FedEx Freight proof-of-delivery documents when applicable.
  • Alerts your team when a POD shows damage, shortage, or other discrepancies.

Customer Invoicing

  • Creates and completes customer invoices in ShipPrimus using the correct customer rate.
  • Sends professional customer invoice emails with Innovative Carriers payment instructions.
  • Supports a reviewer approval step before a customer email is sent — with a full load summary and one-click approve/reject links.
  • Handles power-only loads with unit numbers on the bill-to reference field.
  • Holds customer email when a truckload POD is still missing — carrier billing continues, but your customer is not invoiced prematurely.

When Jerry Needs Your Team

Jerry automates the routine and surfaces the exceptional. When something needs a person, the load dispatcher receives a clear email — with accounting copied — and a direct button to resolve it.

Customer and rate checks

  • No customer on the load — Jerry notifies the dispatcher to enter the customer in ShipPrimus or use the self-service form to set the customer name and rate.
  • Missing customer rate — Same path with a one-click form to enter the rate and resume billing automatically.
  • Low profit on a load — Jerry flags loads below your minimum profit threshold so dispatch can review customer rate vs. carrier cost.

Broker commission support

  • When profit margin falls below your company threshold, Jerry works to align broker commission to your standard low-margin rate — with special handling for designated brokers who are exempt from rate changes.
  • Accounting is notified when a commission adjustment cannot be completed automatically.
  • Insurance premiums added to a load trigger a fresh margin review so commission stays consistent.

Additional charges & accessorials

  • Detects lumper, detention, and other accessorial charges on carrier invoices.
  • Routes four-option approval requests (pay carrier, bill customer, dispute, etc.) when a charge needs a human decision.
  • Skips unnecessary dispatcher notifications when the carrier total already matches Primus.
  • Supports dispute and resolution workflows when a charge is rejected.

Insurance allocation

  • Processes Redkik cargo insurance premium spreadsheets and invoice PDFs.
  • Allocates premiums to shipments and posts insurance charges to each load in ShipPrimus.
  • Sends reconciliation summaries showing posted, skipped, and manual-review items.

Carrier Payables & QuickBooks

  • Marks shipments delivered in ShipPrimus when billing criteria are met.
  • Advances carrier bill approval through your established workflow.
  • Syncs carrier payables to QuickBooks so your books stay current with operations.

Operations & Visibility

  • Weekly undelivered shipment report — Dispatchers receive loads picked up more than two weeks ago with no delivery date.
  • Stuck workflow monitoring — Jerry watches for invoices that stopped mid-process.
  • Dashboard — One place to monitor operations: Gmail connection status, statistics (day / week / month / year), invoices processed (including loads with additional charges), recent invoices, AI-summarized activity logs with “show more,” and an open-task list for items forwarded for review (additional charges, signed POD requests, and similar).
  • Task manager — Open items that need Lisa or ops attention appear on the dashboard; dismiss each task when handled so nothing is lost.
  • Support chat — Ask questions about recent activity and get guided answers.
  • Activity logs — AI summaries of each completed workflow for audits and troubleshooting (not raw engine logs).

Shipment Modes Supported

  • Truckload (TL) — POD required; structured carrier follow-up; customer email held until POD is on file.
  • Power Only — Trailer photos and unit-number billing references supported.
  • Drayage — Mode-aware validation and billing steps.
  • LTL / FedEx Freight — Multi-invoice PDF handling, FedEx Freight POD retrieval, and carrier-specific rules where configured.

What to Expect in Every Jerry Email

  • A plain-language explanation of what happened and why.
  • Load number, carrier, and relevant dollar amounts.
  • A direct button to fix the issue when action is needed (set customer rate, approve a charge, approve customer email, resume workflow, etc.).
  • Confirmation pages on sensitive actions so nothing happens from an accidental click or email scanner.
  • The right people copied — dispatchers for operational items, accounting for financial exceptions.
