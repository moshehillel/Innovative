End-to-End Workflow

From the moment a carrier email arrives to the moment your books are updated, Jerry follows a consistent, auditable path for every shipment.

1
Email arrives

Jerry reads the inbox, classifies the message (carrier invoice, insurance, POD request, administrative, etc.), and extracts attachments.

2
AI document parsing

PDFs and spreadsheets are parsed for load number, PRO, carrier name, invoice amount, line items, POD images, and supporting receipts.

3
Primus lookup & validation

Jerry finds the shipment in ShipPrimus, compares amounts, checks for duplicates, and validates lumpers and accessorials.

4
Carrier bill posted

Validated carrier bills are uploaded to ShipPrimus; costs are entered and the customer invoice workflow begins.

5
Customer invoice & email

Customer invoices are created, reviewed when configured, and emailed with Innovative Carriers payment instructions.

6
Payables & QuickBooks

Carrier payables advance through approval; data syncs to QuickBooks so accounting stays current with operations.

Carrier Invoice Intake

Intelligent document reading

  • Reads carrier invoices from PDF and email attachments — including multi-invoice packets (e.g. LTL statement cover pages followed by individual freight bills).
  • Extracts load number, PRO, carrier BOL, order number, PO, shipment reference, invoice amount, dates, and line-item charges.
  • Matches each document to the correct shipment in ShipPrimus using multiple lookup keys when the load number alone is not present.
  • Flags amount mismatches, duplicate submissions, missing proof documents, and unrecognized charges before billing proceeds.

Multi-key load matching

When a carrier bill references a BOL, order number, or PO instead of an Innovative load number, Jerry attempts resolution in ShipPrimus using every available reference field — reducing manual matching work for your team.

Reference on invoice What Jerry does
Load number Primary lookup — matched directly to ShipPrimus booking.
PRO number Secondary lookup when load number is missing or incorrect.
Carrier BOL / order / PO Additional keys used to find the correct shipment when needed.
No match found Forwarded to billing review with extracted data for manual resolution.

Rate & Amount Validation

  • Compares the carrier bill to the booked carrier cost in ShipPrimus.
  • Subtracts lumper charges from the invoice total before comparing base freight to the Primus rate.
  • Auto-approves charges already reflected in the Primus carrier cost when totals match.
  • Pauses and notifies dispatch when numbers do not align — with load number, carrier, and dollar amounts in plain language.
  • Flags loads with profit below the $10 minimum threshold so dispatch can review customer rate vs. carrier cost.

Duplicate & re-submission protection

  • Detects when the same carrier invoice has already been processed for a load.
  • Prevents double-posting of carrier costs and duplicate customer invoice lines.

Proof of Delivery (POD)

Jerry treats proof of delivery as a gatekeeper for billing. Where a POD is required, your team gets a clear notice — not a surprise later in the process.

  • Detects when a load is missing a POD in ShipPrimus before customer invoicing.
  • Uploads POD documents from carrier emails directly onto the booking.
  • Supports truckload, power-only, and drayage workflows with mode-appropriate rules.
  • For truckload shipments without a POD, Jerry sends structured follow-up reminders to the carrier on a business-day schedule before escalating to your team.
  • Retrieves FedEx Freight proof-of-delivery documents when applicable.
  • Alerts Lisa and dispatch when a POD shows damage, shortage, or other discrepancies.
  • Creates dashboard tasks for signed-POD requests that need ops attention.

Customer Invoicing

  • Creates and completes customer invoices in ShipPrimus using the correct customer rate.
  • Sends professional customer invoice emails with Innovative Carriers payment instructions.
  • Supports a reviewer approval step before a customer email is sent — with a full load summary and one-click approve/reject links.
  • Handles power-only loads with unit numbers on the bill-to reference field.
  • Holds customer email when a truckload POD is still missing — carrier billing continues, but your customer is not invoiced prematurely.
  • Reuses existing issued invoices in Primus when present — preventing duplicate draft invoices and double freight lines.

Broker Commission Rules

When a load's profit falls below your company's 10% threshold, Jerry evaluates whether the broker's commission rate should be adjusted automatically in ShipPrimus.

Important: Profit % vs. GP%
Jerry uses the Profit % shown in the Primus invoice summary (profit divided by actual cost) — not GP% (profit divided by revenue). These are different numbers on the same load. Broker decisions always follow Primus Profit %.
Scenario Jerry's action
Profit % ≥ 10% No commission change — load proceeds normally.
Profit % < 10%, broker has 10% variant Automatically swaps broker to the 10% sales-rep code in ShipPrimus.
Profit % < 10%, Karen Adams Exempt — commission is never changed for designated brokers.
Profit % < 10%, no 10% variant exists Lisa is notified; invoicing continues without blocking the load.
Insurance premium posted to load Fresh Profit % review runs after the premium is added.

Additional Charges & Accessorials

  • Detects lumper, detention, reweigh, and other accessorial charges on carrier invoices.
  • Routes four-option approval requests when a charge needs a human decision: pay carrier, bill customer, dispute, or other resolution paths.
  • Skips unnecessary dispatcher notifications when the carrier total already matches Primus.
  • Requires supporting receipts for certain charge types before auto-approval.
  • Creates dashboard tasks for open additional-charge items so nothing is lost between emails.
  • Supports dispute and resolution workflows when a charge is rejected.

Cargo Insurance (Redkik)

  • Processes Redkik cargo insurance emails with invoice PDF, ACH authorization, and Excel allocation spreadsheet.
  • Extracts invoice number and total premium from the insurance invoice PDF.
  • Allocates premiums across shipments listed in the spreadsheet and posts insurance charges to each load in ShipPrimus.
  • Skips loads that already have insurance posted (idempotent re-runs).
  • Sends reconciliation summaries showing posted, skipped, and manual-review items.
  • Triggers a broker commission Profit % review after insurance is posted to each load.

Special Email Handlers

Not every email is a carrier invoice. Jerry recognizes several special message types and handles them without forwarding noise to your team.

Apex / administrative

Recognizes administrative broadcast emails and marks them as intentionally ignored — no false billing alerts.

POD requests

When someone requests a signed POD, Jerry looks up the load and sends it from ShipPrimus — or escalates to Lisa when needed.

eModal broadcasts

Port and drayage system notifications are classified and ignored when they are not actionable invoices.

Operations Dashboard

Your team has a live operations dashboard at advancedautomations.net — one place to monitor Jerry's activity across Innovative Carriers.

Dashboard features

  • Gmail connection status — confirms Jerry is connected and polling your invoice mailbox.
  • Statistics — invoices processed by day, week, month, and year; includes loads with additional charges.
  • Recent invoices — quick view of the latest completed and in-progress workflows.
  • AI-summarized activity logs — human-readable summaries of each workflow (not raw engine logs), with "show more" for detail.
  • Open tasks — items forwarded for review: additional charges, signed POD requests, human-review escalations, and similar.
  • Dismiss tasks — mark each task handled when resolved so the queue stays clean.
  • Support chat — ask questions about recent activity and get guided answers.

What to Expect in Every Jerry Email

  • A plain-language explanation of what happened and why — written for dispatch and accounting, not engineers.
  • Load number, carrier name, and relevant dollar amounts clearly displayed.
  • A direct button to fix the issue when action is needed (set customer rate, approve a charge, approve customer email, resume workflow, etc.).
  • Confirmation pages on sensitive actions so nothing happens from an accidental click or email scanner.
  • The right people copied — dispatchers for operational items, Lisa and accounting for financial exceptions.
  • Profit % (not GP%) shown on broker commission notifications when a percentage is included.
